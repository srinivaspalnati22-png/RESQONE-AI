# 🛡️ RESQONE AI+ — Complete End-User & Responder Guide

Welcome to **RESQONE AI+**, an offline-first, AI-driven emergency intelligence ecosystem designed to save lives during critical incidents. This guide details how the application works and how to use every feature as a citizen, bystander, or emergency responder.

---

## 🌟 1. System Overview & Core Philosophy

In traditional emergency response systems, users face **fragmentation**: separate apps for calling an ambulance, finding blood donors, accessing snakebite first aid, or contacting family. During high-stress crises, every second lost toggling apps can be fatal.

**RESQONE AI+** unifies five emergency domains into one single platform:
1. **AI Emergency Copilot**: Natural language text and voice emergency triage with transparent reasoning.
2. **Automatic Crash Detection & Telemetry**: Multi-signal sensor monitoring (18.4G acceleration threshold + audio impact detection).
3. **Snakebite Emergency AI**: Species identification, venom toxicity analysis, and antivenom (AVS) stock tracking.
4. **Smart Blood Donor Matching**: ABO/Rh blood compatibility matrix filtering and GPS proximity ranking.
5. **Hospital Resource Intelligence & Mission Control**: Live ICU bed availability, hospital readiness, and offline sync.

---

## 📱 2. How to Use RESQONE AI+ as a User / Citizen

### 🚨 Scenario A: Reporting an Emergency (Voice or Text)
1. **Navigate to the "AI Copilot" tab** (or click **"REPORT EMERGENCY NOW"** on the home screen).
2. **Choose input method**:
   - **Voice**: Tap the microphone icon and speak in English, Telugu (తెలుగు), or Hindi (हिंदी). Example: *"A rider crashed at MG Road, heavy bleeding and unconscious!"*
   - **Text**: Type the details into the prompt field and click **"Triage & Dispatch"**.
   - **Preset Scenarios**: Tap **"Judges Demo Mode"** to load instant test emergencies like *Spectacled Cobra Bite* or *Multi-Vehicle Crash*.
3. **Review AI Triage Output**:
   - **Severity Rating**: Level 1 (Minor) to Level 4 (Critical/Life-Threatening).
   - **AI Confidence Score**: E.g., 94.5% certainty rating.
   - **Explainability Card**: View exact reasons extracted by the AI (e.g. *head trauma, arterial bleeding, unconsciousness*).
   - **Action Guidance**: Step-by-step instructions on what to do while waiting for help.
4. **Scroll-Scrubbed Rescue Journey**:
   - As you scroll down the page, watch the **3D rescue vehicle video** smoothly advance along its GPS route towards the location.

---

### 🐍 Scenario B: Snakebite Emergency & Antivenom Triage
1. **Open the "Snakebite AI" module**.
2. **Provide Details / Attributes**:
   - Select bite location, snake appearance (e.g. *hood mark, yellow-black bands*), or upload a photo of the snake.
3. **Instant Species Identification**:
   - The AI identifies species (e.g., **Spectacled Cobra (Naja naja)** vs **Russell's Viper** vs **Common Krait**).
   - Classifies venom type: **Neurotoxic** (paralysis risk) vs **Hemotoxic** (bleeding risk).
4. **Antivenom (AVS) Locator**:
   - Locates nearest hospitals with verified Polyvalent Antivenom Serum in stock (e.g. *Victoria Hospital - 14 Vials available*).
   - Auto-reserves antivenom vials and displays emergency navigation route.

---

### 🩸 Scenario C: Requesting Urgent Blood & Locating Donors
1. **Open the "Smart Blood Donor" module**.
2. **Select Recipient Blood Group**: E.g. **O Negative (O-)**.
3. **Medical ABO/Rh Compatibility Engine**:
   - Automatically calculates universal compatibility rules (e.g., O- can receive O-, AB+ can receive all).
4. **GPS Distance & Speed Ranking**:
   - Views nearby verified donors ranked by proximity (e.g. *Rahul S. - 1.2 km away*).
5. **Dispatch Courier / Request Blood**:
   - Trigger emergency blood courier delivery or contact verified donors with 1 tap.

---

### 🚗 Scenario D: Automatic Crash Detection
1. **Background Sensor Monitoring**:
   - The app uses device accelerometer and audio signals to detect violent deceleration (>15G) and impact sounds.
2. **30-Second Safety Countdown**:
   - If a crash occurs, a loud alert activates with a 30-second countdown.
   - If the user is uninjured, they tap **"I AM SAFE"** to cancel.
3. **Automated SOS Dispatch**:
   - If no response occurs, GPS coordinates, medical history, and emergency contacts are automatically notified via SMS/Data, and the nearest ICU center is dispatched.

---

### 📴 Scenario E: Offline Resilience Mode
1. **No Cell Service / Off-Grid Usage**:
   - RESQONE AI+ functions as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with local offline storage (IndexedDB).
2. **Offline Data Access**:
   - Offline Profile, Emergency Contacts, First-Aid Protocols, and Snakebite Guide remain fully accessible offline.
3. **Background Sync**:
   - Any SOS requests submitted while offline are queued locally and automatically synced to emergency servers as soon as internet connectivity returns.

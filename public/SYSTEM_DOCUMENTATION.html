1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

RESQONE AI+ is a unified, offline-first emergency intelligence and multimodal rescue coordination ecosystem. It bridges the critical "Golden Hour" gap during life-threatening crises by automating:

  • Zero-Touch Crash Detection: Continuous 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope physics monitoring that triggers rescue even if victims are unconscious.
  • Polyvalent Antivenom (AVS) Logistics: Image/symptom species classification linked with real-time vial inventory at nearby tertiary hospitals.
  • Hyper-Local Blood Mesh: Cryo-courier temperature tracking and instant ABO/Rh match dispatch within a 15 km radius.
  • Real-Time 108 CAD Integration: Automated green-corridor ambulance routing and ICU trauma bay bed reservations.

3. Page-by-Page & Button-by-Button Documentation

🏠 Page 1: Home Command Hub (LandingPage.jsx)

  • Microphone Button (Mic): Starts speech recognition in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, or English.
  • NLP Emergency Search Bar: Evaluates natural-language typed or spoken emergencies and automatically navigates to the matching sector.
  • Quick Action Chips (🩸 O- Blood, 🐍 Snake Bite AVS, 🚗 108 Crash): Instant 1-tap sector triggers.
  • 4 Department Navigation Cards: Instant entry to 3D Crash, Blood Donor, Snakebite AI, and Hospital ER modules.

🚗 Page 2: 3D Crash Detection & Accident Rescue (AccidentPage.jsx)

  • 3D Three.js Vehicle Canvas: Interactive chassis rotation, roll/pitch/yaw visualization, and crumple-zone inspection.
  • Simulation Buttons (Simulate 4.85G Crash & Simulate Side-Impact T-Bone): Injects physics telemetry spikes to test automated workflows.
  • "Dispatch ALS-108 Ambulance" Button: Generates green-corridor CAD navigation for ambulance AP-TRAUMA-99.
  • "Reserve Trauma ICU Bed" Button: Reserves ICU Trauma Bay #4 at GGH Vijayawada.
  • "Send SMS to 5 Family Contacts" Button: Sends SMS alerts with live GPS tracking links.

🩸 Page 3: Universal Blood Bank & Donor Mesh (BloodDonorPage.jsx)

  • ABO/Rh Blood Group Filters (O-, O+, A-, A+, B-, B+, AB-, AB+): Queries live donor availability.
  • "Request Immediate Blood Courier" Button: Dispatches a temperature-controlled (4°C) cryo-courier drone/bike.
  • "Register as Voluntary Blood Donor" Button: Opens onboarding modal to register as an emergency donor.
  • "Toggle Availability (🟢 Available / ⚪ Busy)" Button: Toggles donor active status in the matching pool.

🐍 Page 4: Snakebite AI & Antivenom Locator (SnakebitePage.jsx)

  • "Take Photo / Upload Snake Image" Button: Runs computer vision species classification (Spectacled Cobra, Russell's Viper, Saw-scaled Viper, Common Krait).
  • Symptom Triage Checklist: Toggles Rapid Swelling, Ptosis, Bleeding, Respiratory Distress.
  • "Calculate Required AVS Dosage" Button: Computes initial pediatric/adult antivenom vial dosage.
  • "Find Nearest AVS Stocked Hospital" Button: Maps tertiary hospitals with verified antivenom cold-storage.
  • "Call Hospital Emergency Room Direct" Button: 1-touch telephone dialer to the ER on-duty toxicologist.

📊 Page 5: Role-Specific Mission Control Dashboard (DashboardPage.jsx)

  • Citizen Mode: Personal SOS tracker, family SMS dispatch, assigned 108 ambulance ETA (Accept button hidden for safety).
  • Hospital ER Mode: Trauma Bay ICU bed allocations (12 Available), Polyvalent AVS cold-storage (395 Vials), surgery suites with an "Admit Emergency Patient" button.
  • 108 Rescue Mode: Advanced Life Support unit telemetry with an "Activate Green Corridor" button.
  • Blood Bank Mode: Regional ABO/Rh inventory with a "Dispatch Cryo-Courier" button.
  • First Responder Mode: 3.5km local CAD emergency radius with an "Accept Volunteer Mission" button.

👤 Page 6: Authentication, Google OAuth & Profiles (AuthPage.jsx)

  • Stakeholder Role Selector: Citizen, Hospital ER, 108 Rescue, Blood Donor, or Volunteer.
  • "Continue with Google" Button: Supabase Google OAuth 2.0 with dynamic origin redirect.
  • "1-Tap Quick Demo Access" Button: Instant guest authorization for evaluation.
  • "Next: Setup 5 Family SOS Contacts" & "Complete Registration" Buttons: Responsive form submission with safe-area bottom clearance.
